Fraunces-latin.woff2 is a subset of packages/gnomon-gui/static/fonts/Fraunces-opsz-wght.woff2
(Fraunces, SIL Open Font License 1.1, OFL-Fraunces.txt beside this file). Made 2026-11-23 with fontTools:

  fonttools varLib.instancer Fraunces-opsz-wght.woff2 wght=400:600 opsz=20:144 -o fr.ttf
  pyftsubset fr.ttf --unicodes="U+0020-007E,U+00A0,U+00A9,U+00B7,U+2013,U+2014,U+2018,U+2019,U+201C,U+201D,U+2026" \
    --layout-features='kern,liga,locl,ccmp' --no-hinting --desubroutinize --flavor=woff2 --output-file=Fraunces-latin.woff2

It holds Basic Latin and the punctuation the pages use, weights 400 to 600, optical sizes 20 to 144.
The pages set Fraunces at 21px and larger, in weights 400, 500 and 600. A character outside the subset
falls back to Georgia. The JetBrains Mono files are copied unchanged.
