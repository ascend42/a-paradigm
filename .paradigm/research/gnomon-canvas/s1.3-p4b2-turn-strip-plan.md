# S1.3 P4b-2 item 1: the latest turn under the board ("show chat"), and Esc from the composer

Author: Arky (architect), 2026-11-24. Read-only plan; Luna rules the open questions before the build. Task T-2026-11-25-001.

## Why it breaks today
- The dock covers the chat from the pane top to the feed bottom (`packages/gnomon-gui/src/ui/board-dock.ts:114-117`) and makes the header, viewport, empty sheet and jump key inert (`:98-101`). Nothing outside the feed draws a turn, so the sent message and the reply land in the hidden viewport.
- Esc from the composer is filtered out by `!editableFocus()` (`src/ui/app.ts:4579`; true for the textarea, `app.ts:2527-2530`).

## 1. What the person sees and does
Grounded: `.atelier/systems/gnomon/design-system.yaml:1176` ("under the leaf, always visible: the turn's working line (transcript-row's working key, with 'show chat' which folds the leaf) and the composer"); `DESIGN.md:77` ("so you keep talking while it is open"); `docs/specs/gnomon.md:1531` (Esc or the head's esc key folds the leaf). `design-system.yaml:1197` chose the working line as the ONLY view of the chat; the gates found it too thin (the working row is gone when the turn ends, `app.ts:298-299`). Changing that is Luna's call.

Proposed default: a strip of at most three one-line rows, 24px each, under the leaf.
- (a) you: `you · <message, one line, cut with …> · hh:mm`; `waiting` when queued behind a running turn; `not sent · <why>` in `--fail` when a `send.refused` row follows.
- (b) reply: `<Claude|local model> · <latest assistant item of this turn, one line, …>`. Streaming is per assistant message (the store hides token partials, `src/core/store.ts:1230`). After the turn ends the row takes the result tone (warn/fail).
- (c) working key (`workingEl` markup, `app.ts:533-551`) only while the turn runs, with a quiet `show chat` key at the right end of the last row.
- Live choices, offer slips, host slips and run rows are never copied; if the turn holds any, the reply row ends `· more in the chat`.
- Room: 3 rows at >=300px board room, 2 at >=200px, else only (b) or (c) with show chat. The strip never hides.
- Show chat = fold (as recorded); reopen with the same hash by edge tab or Alt+B; focus goes to `#viewport` at the latest row (`#jump-latest` path, `app.ts:4123`). The working key does the same and leaves its step list unfolded in the chat. On a held-still board holding words/unsaved changes, show chat does not fold; it repeats `leaf.sayHeld()`.
- Esc from the composer folds the board; focus stays in the composer. Runs after the slash menu (`slash-menu.ts:960-964`), dictation grace (`app.ts:4570`) and stop-speaking (`app.ts:4583`). Ignored on `e.repeat`, `e.isComposing`, `defaultPrevented`, a modifier, or a focused card.

## Luna's rulings (2026-11-24; decided by Luna, pending founder review)

Recorded in `.atelier/systems/gnomon/design-system.yaml` under `board-leaf`: the anatomy line (was :1176), the new `turn_strip` block, and a new `chose` entry beside the old :1197 one.

1. **Show the you and reply rows: yes.** This overrides :1197. Reason: DESIGN.md says the board stops above the composer "so you keep talking while it is open", and talking needs both sides. The working line is gone once the turn ends. The transcript rail stays rejected, because rows cost height, not width.
2. **Two rows at most, not three.** The you row is 24px. The turn row is 28px because it holds a key: the working key while the turn runs, then the reply line once it ends. Two rows when the board's room measured without the strip is at least 300px, otherwise the turn row alone (the you row goes first). The strip never hides. Reason: at 1024 × 768 with no pinned cards, 538 − 53 leaves 485px, which is more than the rail's 472px. A third row would fold the rail in the common case, and while a turn runs the working key says more than a half-written line.
3. **Words.**
   - you: `you · <message …> · hh:mm`. `waiting` replaces the time while the message is queued.
   - Refused: the chat's own `send.refused` first line, verbatim, in `--fail`, led by ✕. No second wording; its `next:` is in the chat.
   - Turn row once the turn ends: `Claude · <latest assistant line …>` or `local model · …`, in `--ink2`, plain text, no `title=`. An end that is not "done" adds ` · stopped: turn limit` (`--warn-ink`) or ` · error` (`--fail`), with no tokens. An empty reply reads `Claude · no answer`.
   - Tail, never cut: ` · needs you in the chat` when a live choice waits on the person, else ` · more in the chat` for offers, host slips or runs.
   - `show chat` is a key quiet with the title `Show the chat · folds the board; ⌥B reopens it`.
   - Reason: principle 5, and one wording per fact. "Needs you" is the Workshop's word.
4. **Esc from the composer folds the board and focus stays in the composer.** Reason: moving focus to the canvas would turn the next typed letters (N, T, R…) into tool switches. Esc already means fold for every leaf. On a held board, Esc says the held line instead.
5. **Show chat on a held board: as Arky proposed.** It does not fold. The leaf says its held line again, as Esc does. While the board is held, the key's title reads `Kept open: your words are on this page only`. Reason: a fold there would drop words that exist only on the page, and a disabled key would hide why.
6. **A press on the working key: yes.** It does what show chat does, leaves the step list unfolded in the chat, and puts focus on that working key. It follows the same trusted-press and held-board rules. Reason: principle 2, a key must press. The step list (max 40vh) cannot open inside the strip without eating the board.

**Pattern.** This is a new variant, `turn-strip`, of `transcript-row`, not a new component. The working key and the key quiet are reused unchanged. The one-line you and turn rows are new forms of the user and assistant variants, so the variant is proposed by this record. Mika draws it into `specimen-board.html#leaf` and vets it before its CSS ships. The logic (`latestTurn`, `turnStripRows`, the Esc branch) can start now.

**Note for item 6 (telemetry), not ruled here.** In the design, show chat is a fold: the same word and the same edge tab. Whether a quick peek counts as an end is a stats question for that PR.
