# S1.3: conditions binding passes P3b and P4

Collected 2026-11-24 from the reviews of P1 (PR #112), P2 (the mod) and P3a (PR #113). These bind the builder of each pass and are checked at its review. The design is `s1.3-draft.md`.

## P3b (board handle, paste, engine patches, leaf wiring)

From Aegis:
- `gestureOf` is called only with the listener's own Event and `Date.now()`; never an object literal, never `event.timeStamp`. No production caller passes `now` to `sendModCommand`.
- `sendModCommand` receives the `GnomonClient` instance itself, never a wrapper, from one bundled copy of `gesture.ts`.
- `elementsToOps` gets as `prev` exactly the state the engine was last rendered from. `render()` never fires `onEdit`. Test: a remote add or move arriving mid-drag yields no delete or update of that node.
- `engineOf` returns handle-minted ids, never the node id.
- Agent and unknown marks of kind text, stroke and frame are visibly distinguished in the rendered output, asserted by test (the engine may ignore stroke style on those types).
- On `cursor-epoch` reset, link loss or dimension switch, build a new `DimLive`; undo entries never outlive the log.
- `DimClipboard.clear()` runs on window blur, on unmount, and on any copy or cut not originating from the board.
- `lastDimOf` and `list(repo)` are display hints, never access decisions. `stats().dropped > 0` shows a visible line.
- A5: paste is intercepted by containment and the engine's paste handler never runs; the C8 test adds rich HTML, a file and an IME composition pasted into the text-edit textarea.
- The dimension mod ships no view until the host verifies a view's `hsid` for first-party mods.

From Mika:
- Diamond and arrowheads: decide them and make `normal.ts` and `dim-map.ts` agree (applying the S1.1 handle's rule to the map's output today writes two `update {head: null}` ops and deletes the diamond).
- Complete every element through `normalOf` before the engine sees it.
- Sticky, stub and hand are read from the rendered state only.
- Stub words get one owner (the map's label is uncut and the overlay would say the kind twice).
- A stand-in clipboard line pasted with nothing held adds nothing and says so.
- Mint the gesture stamp at the END of a gesture (stamps expire after 10 seconds).
- Goldens include a labelled right-to-left arrow and a stroke drawn at 400%.

Added after the P3a fix batch (PR #113, head 7ea2086c):
- The handle sends a diff ONLY on a real edit, never on a selection, a pan or a render. A board holding a malformed `z` key gets `z` updates from any diff, with no edit made: sent on a selection, that is a save and an undo entry whose Cmd+Z visibly does nothing.
- A copy that had words, pasted after a window blur (the register cleared), comes back as bare text. Decide what the leaf does and have Aegis confirm.
- F5's test pins three named disagreements between `normal.ts` and `dim-map.ts`; it would not notice a fourth. The P3b golden must cover the full kind and style matrix through the real engine.
- Send spacing is per dimension (50 ms). The host limit of 20 appends per second is per mod: put one shared limiter where the controllers are created.
- Pass `Session.connId` as `DimLive`'s `deps.connId`, so another tab of the same principal cannot confirm a batch.
- `pin` the open dimension in `DimStore` on open and `unpin` on close; show a visible line for a `partial` listing (an evicted dimension written again is held only from that row on).
- Tick `DimLive.timeout()` at `nextDeadline()`; that also releases a queued send.
- `dim.op` applies partially and the size check is on everything sent: a batch too large as a whole is refused whole.

From Probe and the mod builder:
- A freehand stroke over 2,048 points (and any row over 16 KiB or 4,096 values) is refused whole. Split or simplify long strokes before sending; never lose a scribble silently.
- The host allows 20 appends per second per mod: the sender spaces sends.
- Windows: the clipboard may return CRLF; a multi-mark paste must still match.
- `dim.open` on a known dimension always appends a start row: do not call it on every reconnect.
- The `Canvas{ds}` to `dim` rename lands here (it touches the GUI bundle).

## P4 (leaf in the app)

- The keep line reads both `keep` and `link`; "saved with the link down" is a real state.
- Notices use plain words from a table, never `code`, `field` or `why`; `lost` counts changes; a fail toast does not cover the head's keys.
- The Dimensions list prints the time from `lastAt`.
- The spec states that undo of a delete and paste of a copy both re-author as the person, including agent-written words; it uses "gesture stamp" and Aegis's A1 sentence verbatim; §11.5 G-GUI-04 gains "and `mod.command` is sent only by the one sender".
- Missing from the controller or the design, to settle with Luna: redo, a read-only board, an ended dimension, the word for an unknown kind, stub size, the clipboard line ("1 mark from a Gnomon board" proposed).
- G-DDL-08 in full, with Aegis, Mika and Probe gating.

- C6 write grants per hsid.

- Regenerate Appendix A in full (seven rows still differ from the checker).

## Added after the review of P3b-1 (PR #116, head 2c21b611): binds P3b-2

From Aegis (the first item is a merge condition for the next PR):
- `syntheticInput` must not be a runtime option in the shipped chunk. Make it a build-time define that is false in `scripts/board-build.mjs`, so the branch is removed from the pinned chunk; add a test that the shipped `board/board.js` does not contain the token; the P12 harness runs its CSP checks on the pinned bytes and its edit checks on a probe build (or drops its edit checks; the Chrome tests cover those with trusted input).
- `drift()` is diagnostic only and is never sent, held by a test.
- `pasteUntil` is not cleared by `render()` or `putBack`; it goes away with paste interception.
- Narrow `mine` (the marks an edit may report) to held or selected marks, their labels, bound arrows and frame children.
- On the third put-back with no input the engine's form stands un-dirty; a later real edit of that mark carries the engine's delta. Close it.
- One handle per dimension; on dimension switch or `cursor-epoch` reset, unmount and remount.
- Paste, copy, cut and alt-drag duplicate are contained before the engine.
- Pasted or remote text and point counts are bounded before the map reads them.

From Kit:
- A split stroke is several `add` ops in one `onEdit` call: the sender puts pieces in separate rows when their sum exceeds 16 KiB.
- Refusal listeners are still module-wide (the engine calls them with no instance).
- A press on a fallback stub reports refusal `{what: 'fallback', why: 'kept-as-it-is'}`; the leaf's line for it needs Luna.

From Mika (S1.4, before agents draw): an agent's stroke and frame carry the hand along their length; decide what seals do below about 50% zoom (T-2026-11-24-009).

Standing: the real-engine board tests are skipped on CI; the builder and the orchestrator each run `npm test` in `packages/gnomon-gui` and the P12 probe locally before a merge.

From Mika's rendered verification of the P3b-1 fixes (none blocking):
- The stub refusal line stays through a press on empty ground and through selecting another mark; clear it on the next press or key. At 88 characters it will likely be cut in the 423 px leaf: check at 1024.
- The unknown hand's 8 px hollow pin floats about 13 px from its stroke and reads as detached; half that gap.
- A frame narrower than about 80 px: the seal meets a long title.
- A split scribble is several adds in one edit: its pieces are packed by fit (pieces that fit one row together share it; accepted at the review of PR #118) and undone together.

## Added after the review of P3b-2 (PR #117, head 51239989): binds P4 (the leaf live in `app.ts`)

From Aegis:
- N1: paste length is checked before sanitising (`src/board/index.ts` ~944); glyphs lengthen the text, so `dimPasteTextOp` can emit more than 4,000 characters and fail silently at the validator. Check after sanitising and refuse `too-long`.
- N2: `chromeField` knows only text and search inputs (~903). Exempt any input, textarea, select or contenteditable in the chrome.
- N3: a copy of selected chrome text (the title, the keep line) is swallowed and gives the board's marks. Leave the copy alone when the selection is non-collapsed in the chrome outside the host.
- `select()` skips fallback stubs.
- `parent` kept to an uncopied frame is accepted: update the A6 text at `src/core/dim-clipboard.ts:16` and in the spec (both still say "dropped otherwise"); tests for a frame deleted before the paste, another dimension, and a clone landing outside the frame (all drop `parent`); `landsIn` (~96-101) ignores `points`: use `boxOf`.
- A test that a copy holding ESC, a bidi control and a variation selector still matches itself on paste.
- `chrome` is only ever the `bd-leaf` element, never a pane or the body; a test that a paste with focus in a card or the composer reaches no board listener.
- An edit whose cause yields no stamp (null, expired, repeat) is put back with `render(prev)` and said in the leaf, never left as a local-only change.
- `onOps` and `onEdit` go to the ONE sender; `gestureOf(cause, Date.now())` is called at send time with the handle's own event; no `now` argument is passed to `sendModCommand`.
- The leaf, and so its `DimClipboard`, is destroyed on dimension switch, `cursor-epoch` reset and link loss. Clones never cross dimensions.
- `o.view()` returns the `DimLive` view of the same dimension the handle renders.
- A paste of 64 clones respects the ops-per-row cap and the 16 KiB row split; a split stroke's pieces are packed by fit (amended at the review of PR #118: not forced one per row); undo takes all rows of one paste or one split stroke in one step.
- The probes CI job installs dependencies for P12: keep that job `pull_request` with `contents: read` and no secrets.
- Before S1.4: an agent's words must not reach a terminal with control characters (now covered by the display sanitizer on copy; keep it).

From Mika:
- Three lines are cut off in the 423 px leaf (about 59 characters fit). Use: "One mark holds 4,000 characters · paste or type less"; "6 marks were left out · a copy takes 64 at most"; "Nothing was cut · a cut takes 64 marks at most".
- The paste step never resets while the view is still (a seventh paste sits 96 px off centre), and a second clone paste after panning stacks exactly on the first: reset the step on any other action; step clone pastes too.
- A rail-to-board drag is announced twice in the live region.
- A paste made while a drawn mark is not yet stored is said, not dropped.
- A refused paste says so and leaves no mark; the copy register survives clicking another Gnomon pane and is tested against a real app switch; one file-drop guard covers the whole window, in the wrapper and in a tab; copy between two dimensions is decided (Aegis: clones never cross).
- Arrows whose `parent` is a frame do not move with the frame (pre-existing): answer it.
- For Luna: do the leaf's refusal lines take `next:` (DESIGN.md rule 5); should Cmd+D duplicate through the register; should copy or cut say anything; pasted JSON or SVG as one tall text or shortened; a cut pasted back in place or at +16; "held" in the clipboard line.

From Probe (tests still owed): the leaf's window blur and outside-copy clearing; paste mid-gesture, right after mount, after unmount, with two boards; an outside input untouched while a board is live; whitespace-only and NUL pastes; the `drop` handler's file-name classification has no real-engine test (a real drag never fires `drop` after a refused dragover).

## Added after the review of P4a (PR #118, head a976a152): binds P4b (mounting in `app.ts`), the last pass

From Aegis (final):
- Pass the `GnomonClient` itself to `DimSessions.connect` on every new socket; wire `onClose` to `linkDown` and `cursor-epoch` to `reset()`; never reuse a client object across sockets.
- `/board`, `/boards`, the menu and the Dimensions list open only on their own trusted event; a press on an ended dimension says it will reopen it (`dim.open` un-ends it).
- A session still opening at link loss is not resumed, though its `dim.open` may have landed: the Dimensions list must show that dimension.
- A switch uses `leave()` and says what `lost` reports.
- The check display reads "checked up to" a point whenever `checkedSeq < seq`, never a bare "verified"; `verified: null` is shown as unchecked. (S1.4: put `hash` on the `dim.op` ok answer.)
- N1: the `not-undoable` line must not state a limit the person did not exceed (50 shapes with 20 bound arrows): use a line without the number when the op count is 64 or fewer.
- A command that left, timed out as `no-ack` and may have been written: the line says "may not have been saved".
- Typed text at link loss, option (b) allowed: words held in page memory only, bounded to the text limit and passed through the paste sanitizer; put back only into an open, uncommitted edit on the same dimension for the same principal; committed only by a fresh trusted event on the new connection; dropped on `reset()`, a dimension switch, a hash mismatch or a principal change; no `syntheticInput`, no new engine patch (if it needs either, refuse the commit while down and keep the field open instead, and never say "everything here is saved" after a loss).
- G-DDL-08 keeps the kernel negatives (no stamp, a fifth use, a replayed stamp, a dead focus, forged `hsid`/`repo` ignored).
- Not verified by reading: keys reaching the engine's document-level listeners while the leaf is inert; that only the dimension mod can append the dimension row kinds. Test both.

From Mika (final; the typed-text case BLOCKS mounting):
- The typed-text case is closed and held by a real-input test: today the field stays open under the reconnecting line, Esc makes the words vanish with no line, and on return the board says "everything here is saved".
- The fold works in both inert states (link down and out-of-date): today Esc and the esc key do nothing, and the out-of-date line tells the person to fold.
- A refused delete keeps its selection.
- Pan and zoom while held still: let the person look around (rule it; default: allow pan and zoom, refuse edits).
- Copy while down takes nothing and says nothing: say it or allow it.
- "Not saved · reconnecting" shows even when everything on the board is saved: say "reconnecting" alone when nothing is pending.
- "Not saved" never shows as its own keep state; the keep line shows `partial` and `dropped`.
- A real hash mismatch is driven once (the preview has no hook).
- One notice per event; a failure toast stays clear of the head's keys.
- Checked in the wrapper on a paused and throttled real kernel.
- Luna rules the lines and `next:`.
- S1.4: a mark another hand removes mid-drag is said.

From Judge: `forget` is required; amend "one piece per row" to "packed by fit" (done); tests still owed: an engine test for a paste made while a drawn mark is not yet stored; the register surviving a real app switch; B1 through the real engine (the fixture has exactly 64 marks).
