### Finding 2 — `purpose-portal.ts` non-writeAndConfirm handlers still use inline-verify + `err()` envelopes that include the bareId in the error string

- **Category:** improvement
- **Severity:** LOW (non-security — bareId is user-supplied input, not a cross-session leak)
- **Location:** `packages/paradigm-mcp/src/tools/purpose-portal.ts:822, 872, 923, 975, 1021, 1067`
- **Detail:** Handlers NOT wired to `writeAndConfirm` in v5.38.0 (`handleAddAspect`, `handleAddSignal`, `handleAddFlow`, `handleAddGate`, `handleAddState`) still use the v5.37.11-era inline `readPurposeFile(filePath)` re-read + `err()` pattern, with error strings like `` `add_aspect write verification failed: aspect "${bareId}" not found in ${filePath} after write.` ``.
