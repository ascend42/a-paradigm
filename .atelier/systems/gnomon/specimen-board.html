Gnomon: the board medium

Proposed for milestone S1.1, 2026-11-23. Not codified: every entry here is proposed in design-system.yaml (the slip states are contested) until the founder accepts it and Mika has reviewed it rendered. Drawn with the real styles.css; the bd-* classes in this page are the proposal. The marks on the boards below are a drawing of the look, not the engine.

Reviewed rendered by Mika on 2026-11-23, with Aegis's rulings of the same day folded in. Still proposed. Changed in review: the leaf at a 1024 window, a short rail, the percent as a key, heavier night brackets on the chosen tool, the diamond cut at its optical size and the hex cut drawn, the unknown hand, the stub's lead word, and no ⌥B on a live offer. Each is marked where it appears.

1The board leaf

A leaf over the centre column, the same move as Loops, with one difference: it stops above this session's pinned cards and the composer, so you can keep talking while it is open. The head names the dimension, then the medium. The line under it says which project the dimension belongs to, which sessions are attached and how it was opened. The foot lists the hands that have drawn here. Esc folds the leaf to an edge tab.
